About Warp Projects

Warp Projects is an independent studio that publishes open-source projects. That is what we do: build them, document them clearly and publish everything in one place.

Every release here is free to download. You can read what is inside, check the requirements and inspect the project before anything touches your machine — that is the whole point of this site.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything people ask us most, in one place. Still stuck? The Discord is always open.

  • What exactly is Warp Projects?

    Warp Projects is an independent studio that publishes open-source projects. Everything we finish ends up here, free to download and inspect before you install anything.

  • How do I download a project?

    Open the Projects tab, pick a project and read its page: what is included, the version, the platforms and any requirements. The download button is right there — no account and no waiting.

  • Are the projects really free?

    Yes. Every public release on this site is free to download and use. There are no paid tiers, no locked files and no hidden ads — if a project ever changes, it will be clearly marked.

  • Which platforms do you support?

    Most releases target Windows, with occasional web projects that run entirely in the browser. Each project page lists exactly what it supports, so you know before you download.

  • How often are projects updated?

    Whenever there is something worth shipping. Big releases and fixes are announced on our Discord and YouTube, and each project page shows its latest version and date.

  • Something is broken, or I have an idea. Where do I write?

    If you find a bug or have an idea, write to us on Discord. You can post your idea in the general channel and discuss it with the team. For a bug, open a ticket with detailed information and attach a screenshot so we can see exactly what went wrong. Thanks in advance!
